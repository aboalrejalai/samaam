SC-02 Simulated case

From one keypress to a documented refusal

An oncology patient, 62, whose chemotherapy has weakened her kidneys. A night shift, a standard adult dose, and nothing between the console and the scanner that reads her file.

  1. C

    The request arrives

    The record carries serum creatinine of 229.8 µmol/L. The console asks for CTDIvol 18.0 mGy and DLP 900 mGy·cm.

  2. PP

    Preprocessing strips and computes

    Direct identifiers are dropped, and eGFR is computed with CKD-EPI as the Ministry of Health protocol prints it: 19.1.

  3. P

    The policy node checks

    Four deterministic rules run, each citing its knowledge-base record by explicit ID.

  4. 403

    The device refuses

    The scanner is locked. It is a hold, not a prohibition: a named radiologist can authorise the scan, and that name goes into the audit trail.

What gets lost in a busy shift

Four rules read what a tired eye misses. Each one states its basis, because a binding law and a clinical protocol are not the same thing.

  • Dose above the national level18.0 mGy, level 14STATUTORY
  • Renal prophylaxis not orderedeGFR 19.1, threshold 30NATIONAL_PROTOCOL
  • Metformin not held1000 mg BD, still activeNATIONAL_PROTOCOL
  • Nephrotoxic medicationOxaliplatin and ibuprofen PRNNATIONAL_PROTOCOL

A clinical block is a hold. A privacy breach is a refusal.

AUTHORISE

Held for a named doctor

Emergencies do not wait. A named consultant may release the scan, and the release is recorded as EXECUTED_UNDER_OVERRIDE.

PROHIBITED

Refused without exception

No clinician can authorise what the PDPL forbids. An export of patient records abroad ends the session, and the incident goes into the audit trail.

If the model is deleted, every block still happens

The pipeline follows ITU-T Y.3172 node for node. The decision sits in P, plain rule logic. The model only writes the explanation, after the verdict has arrived.

  1. SRCRegulation, record, settings
  2. CDICOM MWL, HL7 FHIR
  3. PPDrops identifiers, computes eGFR
  4. MExplains only
  5. PThe decision, no model
  6. DCitations, audit
  7. SINKDevice, interface

It speaks the language of the radiology department

The gateway answers a scanner's worklist query over DICOM and reads the hospital record over HL7 FHIR, read-only. Thresholds were reviewed by a consultant radiologist, which turned absolute blocks into holds for authorisation.
