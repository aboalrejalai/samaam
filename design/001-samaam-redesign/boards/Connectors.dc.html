Connectors

How a request reaches Samaam, and how its decision reaches the device.

No CT scanner exposes an API to be driven through. It asks, over DICOM, for the work scheduled on it, and whoever answers decides what appears on the technologist’s screen. Samaam is that answer. The device is not modified, updated or replaced.
