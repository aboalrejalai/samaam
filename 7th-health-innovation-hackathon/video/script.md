# Samaam film: spoken script

English, cloned voice (VoxCPM2). Simulated case. Every number below comes from `scenarios/sc-02.json`, `web/src/components/home/home-data.ts`, `README.md` or the certificate.

**l01** This is a simulated case. A woman of sixty two, with cancer. Chemotherapy has weakened her kidneys.

**l02** Her filtration rate, her eGFR, is down to just nineteen.

**l03** It's a tiring night shift. The technologist enters the usual protocol: one hundred and fifty milliliters of contrast, and a radiation dose above the national reference level.

**l04** The settings look normal. For this patient, they could mean acute kidney injury.

**l05** Between the console and the machine, nothing reads her file before the scan.

**l06** Samaam closes that gap. Samaam is the Arabic word for valve.

**l07** It's a smart safety gateway that stands between the radiology technologist, the CT scanner, and the contrast injector.

**l08** It reads kidney function and medications, and compares the settings with Saudi regulations. In our recorded demo, that took twenty nine milliseconds.

**l09** Then it decides: proceed, or hold the request until a named physician authorizes it, with the regulatory reference and its link.

**l10** It holds on dose and renal risk, never on contrast volume alone. And it catches what gets lost in a busy shift: metformin that wasn't stopped, kidney protection that wasn't ordered, medicines that strain the kidneys.

**l11** Most dose management tools analyze what happened after the exposure. Samaam steps in before it.

**l12** The stop decision comes from deterministic rules, not a language model, so no hallucination can change the result. AI only explains the decision in plain language.

**l13** And because emergencies can't wait, Samaam doesn't ban the scan outright. It holds it for a responsible physician, and records that in an audit trail.

**l14** Patient data is different. If a request would break the Personal Data Protection Law, Samaam refuses, with no exception. No doctor can authorize that.

**l15** Today, the prototype runs on simulated data. It speaks the language of radiology departments, DICOM Modality Worklist and HL7 FHIR, and it rests on forty five regulatory records.

**l16** A consultant radiologist reviewed its scenarios and thresholds. That review turned the outright block into a hold for authorization.

**l17** Samaam won third prize at the AI Readiness Hackathon of the International Telecommunication Union, in the Kingdom, in September twenty twenty six.

**l18** Our next goal is to trial Samaam in a real radiology department.

**l19** That way, a night shift mistake becomes a request that stops before it reaches the patient, not an incident discovered after.
